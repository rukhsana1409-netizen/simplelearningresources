About Learning Made Simple

Clear, practical learning resources for young children and the adults who support them.

Our Purpose

Learning Made Simple creates original printable worksheets and learning activities for young children. The current collection focuses on Preschool, Kindergarten, and Grade 1 skills across early math, reading and language, science, communication, routines, and everyday learning.

The resources are designed for parents, caregivers, teachers, and other adults supporting children at home, in classrooms, or in small-group learning. Each resource includes clear previews and printable pages so adults can review an activity before downloading it.

Meet the Creator

Hi, I'm Rukhsana Parveen, the mom behind Learning Made Simple!

My journey began with a simple wish: to make learning easier, more enjoyable, and more meaningful for my own child.

As a parent, I spent time looking for activities that were simple to understand, visually engaging, and not overwhelming. I wanted resources that made learning feel approachable, with clear instructions, colorful illustrations, and plenty of room for children to practice.

That's what inspired me to create Learning Made Simple.

With a background in software engineering and quality assurance, I've always enjoyed organizing ideas, solving problems, and paying attention to the little details. I bring that same approach to developing learning resources.

What started as a way to support my own child's learning has grown into a collection of free printable worksheets that I hope will help other families too.

How Our Resources Are Created

Each resource begins with a clear learning goal. I develop activities guided by Michigan's academic standards and age-appropriate learning expectations, focusing on meaningful practice, simple instructions, and child-friendly visuals.

I review the worksheets for educational accuracy, readability, layout, and ease of use, making adjustments before they're published.

My goal is to create resources that are simple enough for children to enjoy and practical enough for parents to use at home.

I'm still learning and growing alongside my child, and I'm happy to share what I create with other families.

Made with care, from one parent to another. ❤️
