About Learning Made Simple

Clear, practical learning resources for young children and the adults who support them.

Our Purpose

Learning Made Simple creates original printable worksheets and learning activities for young children. The current collection focuses on Preschool and Kindergarten skills across early math, reading and language, science, communication, routines, and everyday learning.

The resources are designed for parents, caregivers, teachers, and other adults supporting children at home, in classrooms, or in small-group learning. Each resource includes clear previews and printable pages so adults can review an activity before downloading it.

Our Design Approach

Each worksheet pack is built around a specific learning goal. Activities use clear directions, age-appropriate tasks, readable page layouts, and familiar examples that help children understand what to do.

Resources may support practice such as counting, letter sounds, early reading, comparing, sorting, communication, routines, and observation. The accompanying resource page explains the learning focus and, where useful, gives adults a short suggestion for introducing or supporting the activity.

Learning Made Simple does not replace individualized teaching or professional advice. Adults can adapt the pace, prompts, and level of support to the needs of the child using the resource.
