## What this pack is

Five pages that teach a first grader what makes a sentence work: it tells a whole idea, it starts with a capital letter, and it ends with a mark. Page 1 asks the child to spot the whole idea. Page 2 fixes broken sentences with a big letter and the right end mark. Page 3 fixes names and dates, with commas in full dates. Page 4 builds sentences from word cards, including a list that needs commas. Page 5 is independent writing about a park picture, with a three-item self-check.

## The five pages (as approved)

**Page 1. Is It a Sentence?** Four watercolor picture panels (dog running, Mia reading, Ben kicking a ball, two birds in a tree). Each panel has two options with circles; the child circles the words that tell a whole idea. Correct answers: "The dog runs.", "Mia reads a book.", "Ben kicks the ball.", "The birds sing." A Remember box at the bottom: "A sentence tells a whole idea. It tells us something that makes sense by itself." Adult line below: "Ask: who is it about, and what happens?"

**Page 2. Start Big, End with a Mark.** Four watercolor rows (sun, red ball, excited child with a new bike, Sam jumping). Each row shows a lowercase prompt and one ruled line. Prompts stay lowercase as intentional errors: "the sun is up", "where is my red ball", "i love my new bike", "sam can jump high". Correct answers: "The sun is up.", "Where is my red ball?", "I love my new bike!", "Sam can jump high." Adult line below: "Say the sentence aloud, then write it with a big letter and a mark."

**Page 3. Names and Dates.** Four watercolor rows (Mia reading, birthday cake, Ava and Noah playing, two-story red brick school building). Each row shows lowercase words with the words needing a capital in bold, and one ruled line. Highlighting covers every word needing a capital, including "school" in row 4. Prompts: "mia likes to read on sunday", "ben was born on march 3 2026", "ava and noah play on friday", "school starts on august 24 2026". Correct answers: "Mia likes to read on Sunday.", "Ben was born on March 3, 2026.", "Ava and Noah play on Friday.", "School starts on August 24, 2026." Instruction: "Fix each sentence. Names and dates start with a big letter. Add a comma between the day and year in full dates. End with the right mark." Adult line below: "Names of people, days, and months all start with a big letter."

**Page 4. Build a Clear Sentence.** Three watercolor rows (cat on a mat, Leo eating an apple, cat plus dog plus bird). Each row has word cards in a shuffled order and one ruled line. Item 1 cards include a capital "The" as the capitalization clue. Item 3 is a list; the child adds the commas themselves (word cards carry NO commas). Correct answers: "The cat sits on the mat.", "Leo eats an apple.", "I see a cat, a dog, and a bird." Item 3 carries the note "Add the commas." Adult line below: "Read the words aloud in order before writing."

**Page 5. Write Your Own Sentences.** One large watercolor park scene shown whole (girl swinging, boy flying a red kite, boy on a red slide, brown dog chasing a yellow ball, apple tree with a blue bird, bench, flowers, butterfly, sun and clouds). Two writing areas (Sentence 1, Sentence 2), each with two ruled lines. A self-check panel with three checkboxes: "Did I start with a capital?", "Did I end with a mark?", "Did I tell a whole idea?" No adult line on this page.

## The review corrections (her direction)

1. **Illustration style, complete replacement (2026-11-19).** The v1 and v2 illustrations were programmatic vector drawings. 2. **Park apples reduced (2026-11-19).** The approved park sample had too many apples on the tree. The rebuilt park (park-scene-v2.jpg) has about half the apples, spaced naturally.
3. **Page 1 Mia illustration moved up (2026-11-19).** Mia's reading picture is a tall portrait and its lower edge reached the answer text. It is drawn slightly smaller and higher (100x80 box at y+118 instead of the standard 100x92 at y+110), so there is clear space between the picture and the "Reads a book" line. The other Page 1 pictures are unchanged.
4. **Page 3 school replaced (2026-11-19).** The first school picture looked like a small house. It was replaced with a recognizable two-story red brick school building (p3-school-v2.jpg): two rows of many windows, a round clock, a flagpole with a blue flag, double doors, stone steps.
5. **Page 3 instruction clarified (2026-11-19).** The first instruction said "Put a comma in the date.", which suggested every sentence needs a date comma. It was changed to "Add the missing comma in each date.", then, as the final text-only correction, to "Add a comma between the day and year in full dates." The final instruction reads in full: "Fix each sentence. Names and dates start with a big letter. Add a comma between the day and year in full dates. End with the right mark."
6. **Page 1 Remember text (2026-11-19, v2).** The Remember box now reads exactly "A sentence tells a whole idea. It tells us something that makes sense by itself." (her wording).
7. **Page 3 highlighting (2026-11-19, v2).** Highlighting was made consistent: every word needing a capital is bold, including "school" in row 4.
8. **Page 4 capital clue (2026-11-19, v2).** Item 1 word cards include a capital "The" as the ordering clue.
9. **Page 5 adult line removed (2026-11-19, v2).** The adult-facing line "Let your child choose what to write about the picture." was removed from the student page.
10. **Parent Guide rebuilt (2026-11-19, v2).** No Name/Date fields, short title fits, answers with bold "Page N:" labels, new "Help a beginning writer" section (4 tips), and a "If your child finds it hard" section (Page 4 tip only).
11. **Individual PDF quality (2026-11-19).** The per-page PDFs are generated by splitting the final bundle losslessly (pypdf page copy), never from screenshots, thumbnails, or compressed previews. All five are Letter size (612x792), each renders pixel-identical to its bundle page at 400 dpi, and the illustrations are high-resolution JPEGs (1000+ ppi at print size, park 363 ppi). Preview PNGs are generated at 300 dpi for presentation. No em dash anywhere.

## Builder and rebuild

**Builder:** `worksheet-generator/build_sentences_that_work.py`. It imports `draw_header` / `draw_footer` / `draw_logo` verbatim from `build_phonics_az.py`; no locked builder was modified. Shared design code is imported, never forked. The builder embeds the JPEG illustrations via a `_draw_image` helper (PIL aspect-preserving fit, `preserveAspectRatio=True`); JPEGs embed as DCT, so the PDF stays small. The old vector `_scene_*` functions remain in the file but are unused. The builder reads the JPEGs from its output directory (`IMG_DIR = OUT_DIR`); to rebuild from a repo checkout, copy the 15 JPEGs into the output directory first.

**Rebuild command** (from a repo checkout, repo-local venv has reportlab, pypdf, and PIL):

```
cd worksheet-generator && ../.venv/bin/python build_sentences_that_work.py
```

This regenerates the 5-page bundle, the 1-page parent guide, the review PDF, and the five per-page PDFs (split losslessly from the bundle) into the output directory. Rebuilt files must match the hashes above byte-for-byte; the approved finals are the lock, do not re-render them for the website.
