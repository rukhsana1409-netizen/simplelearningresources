## Pages and answer keys

1. **Build a Sentence** (cut and paste) — three builds; tiles are
   shuffled within each set and each set forms exactly one sensible
   sentence: *The cat can sit.* / *I can see a hen.* / *The pup can
   run.* Tiles are sized smaller than their placement boxes for glue
   room; work area and dashed CUT-OUT AREA are fully separated.
2. **Make a Sentence Longer** — expand a short sentence with a
   pictured detail (chip gives the exact words), then rewrite the
   whole sentence on a three-line handwriting guide: *The cat can
   nap.* + "on the mat" / *I see a bug.* + "red" / *The pup can
   run.* + "fast" (chip-given, copied not decoded).
3. **Fix the Sentence** — rewrite each broken sentence correctly;
   hints name the kind of fix, corrected sentences are never shown:
   *the sun is up* (capital + end mark), *ican see the pig* (capital
   I + space + end mark), *The hen is red* (end mark).
4. **Finish the Sentence** — write the missing word on a handwriting
   guide built into the blank; per-item word bank, picture decides:
   *A bug is on a ___.* (log; foils rug/bus), *The ___ is red.* (van;
   foils hen/cup), *I can ___ the pup.* (hug; foils sip/run).
5. **Write Your Own Sentence** — two fresh picture prompts (a child
   with a ball outdoors; a garden with a butterfly), two handwriting
   rows each, a Helping Words bank (I · see · can · like · the · is ·
   on · a · ball · play · sun · red) that prescribes nothing, the
   writer's self-check (capital / spaces / end mark), and an adult
   note welcoming invented spelling.

## Conventions

Every modeled sentence uses only the 11 taught common words, true
CVC words, and words established in earlier packs. Handwriting
guides are Kindergarten three-line (top / dashed midline / baseline,
30 pt rows) wherever children write.
