## What this pack is

Four pages that teach the child how an ending changes what a word means: -s and -es for more than one or he, she, it does it; -ing for happening right now; -ed for already happened. Roots are printed in dark navy and endings in teal, so the child can see where the root ends and the ending begins. The ending adds on directly in every word here; no doubling or drop-e rules in this pack. The -es words end in s, ch, sh, or x: buses, boxes, dishes, watches.

## The four pages (as approved)

**Page 1. One or More (guided).** Two sections, plural -s/-es kept apart from verb -s/-es. The picture tells which meaning fits. Explanation box (her exact wording): "Adding -s or -es can mean more than one, or tell what he, she, or it does. cat becomes cats. run becomes runs. Some words need -es: box becomes boxes, dish becomes dishes." More than one: cats, dogs, hens, boxes, dishes, buses. He, she, it does it: runs, jumps, looks, sings, watches. Child reads each word, circles the root, underlines the ending. Adult line (her exact wording): "For the top section, ask 'How many?' For the bottom section, ask 'What does he, she, or it do?'"

**Page 2. Happening Now (-ing, matching).** Six action words on the left, six large matching pictures on the right, one word and one picture per row, with a wide open middle for drawing lines. No matching dots, no printed arrows. Pictures are radius 35 circles (70pt across) with the clearer remade images. Words: jumping, looking, playing, singing, walking, helping. Pictures are shuffled so no word matches straight across: jumping matches the fifth picture down, looking the sixth, playing the first, singing the third, walking the second, helping the fourth. Teaching box: "Adding -ing means the action is happening right now. jump becomes jumping. The ending goes right on the end of the word."

**Page 3. Already Happened (-ed, matching).** Six root words on the left, each with a large supporting picture beside it, and six past-tense words on the right, one root and one past form per row, with a wide open middle for drawing lines. No matching dots, no printed arrows. Pictures are radius 33 circles (66pt across) with the clearer remade images. Roots: jump, look, play, walk, help, wash. Past forms shuffled so no root matches straight across: jump matches jumped (last), look matches looked (fifth), play matches played (second), walk matches walked (first), help matches helped (fourth), wash matches washed (third). Teaching box: "Adding -ed means it already happened. jump becomes jumped. walk becomes walked. The word tells about yesterday."

**Page 4. Choose the Ending (independent).** Six sentences, each with a word missing its ending; the child picks the correct ending from two choices. Exactly one correct answer per item, with grammar, spelling, and subject-verb agreement verified. Approved as-is in v1; unchanged through v4. Note: sentences 5 and 6 use "every day," where -ed (past habitual) is also grammatically possible alongside the intended -s/-es; she approved Page 4 as-is, so no change was made.
