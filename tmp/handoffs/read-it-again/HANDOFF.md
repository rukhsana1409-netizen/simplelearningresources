## What this pack is

Five short decodable passages (31-45 words each), read and reread for fluency: accurate, smooth, expressive reading with understanding. No timed reading, no words-per-minute targets, no speed pressure. Each page has one passage in a large, clear reading box (17pt bold, 28-30pt leading) with one colorful watercolor illustration, one fluency activity, and one brief comprehension check placed after the fluency activity. Fluency stays the primary objective.

Difficulty progresses through the patterns and the independence, not through length. Page 1 is supported (adult models), Pages 2-4 are guided, Page 5 is independent. Passages use only patterns taught in Resources 1-3 (blends; digraphs sh, ch, th, wh, ph; final-e; vowel teams ai, ay, ee, ea, oa, ow as long o only) plus high-frequency words and inflectional -s.

## The five passages (verbatim, as approved)

**Page 1. Read It Smoothly (RF.1.4, supported). Short vowels and blends only. 31 words, 8 sentences.**
Sam has a big dog. The dog is tan. Sam and the dog run to the pond. The dog jumps in. Plop! Sam grins. The dog is wet. Sam is glad.

**Page 2. Scoop the Phrases (RF.1.4, guided). Adds digraphs (sh, th). 45 words, 8 sentences.**
The ship is big. The ship has a red flag. The ship is by the sand. A fish swims by the ship. The fish is fast. The fish swims with a crab. The crab is red. The ship is big and the fish is fast.

**Page 3. Read It with Feeling (RF.1.4, guided). Adds final-e. 38 words, 8 sentences.**
Jake has a red bike. Jake rides the bike on the path. The bike is fast! Jake spots a snake in the grass. 'Is it safe?' Jake asks. The snake is big. Jake rides home. Jake is safe.

**Page 4. Read It Again (RF.1.4, guided to independent). Adds vowel teams. 41 words, 8 sentences.**
It is a rainy day. The rain falls on the grass. The rain taps on the glass. We stay inside and play a game. We play with blocks. We make a tall stack. The rain stops. We go back and play.

**Page 5. Read It on Your Own (RF.1.4, independent). All patterns mixed. 36 words, 8 sentences.**
We plant seeds in a pot. The seeds need sun. The sun is hot. The plants grow tall. We see green leaves. We pick red beans. We eat them at lunch. It is a nice lunch.

## Fluency activities (one per page)

- **P1:** Adult reads aloud first as a model, child reads, child reads again. "My Reading" self-check (tick "I read every word." / "I read it smoothly.") plus reread counter "I read it ___ times."
- **P2:** Child draws a scoop (curved arc) under each group of words that go together, right on the passage. A printed model scoop sits beneath the passage ("Try it like this:" with arcs under "The ship" and "is big."), measured with stringWidth and verified exactly under their phrases. Then the child reads again, pausing at each scoop.
- **P3:** Child finds and circles the ! and the ? in the passage. Reads "The bike is fast!" in an excited voice and "Is it safe?" in a wondering voice. Voice match at the bottom (scrambled): ! to excited voice, ? to question voice, . to calm voice.
- **P4:** Child reads the passage three times, coloring one circle for each read (Read 1, Read 2, Read 3). Then circles what got better: "I read it smoother." / "I read the hard words." / "I used my voice."
- **P5:** Child reads alone, then reads again. Reread counter "I read it ___ times."

## Comprehension checks (one per page, after the fluency activity)

- **P1:** "Where did the dog jump?" Circle: pond / bed / box. Answer: pond.
- **P2:** "What swims by the ship?" Circle: fish / bird / cat. Answer: fish.
- **P3:** "Why did Jake ask, 'Is it safe?'" Oral response only, clearly labeled "Tell an adult." No writing box.
- **P4:** "What did the children play with inside?" Circle: blocks / balls / cars. Answer: blocks.
- **P5:** "What did we eat for lunch?" Circle: beans / cake / fish. Answer: beans.

## Parent Fluency Guide (separate one-page guide, NOT in the student bundle)

One page, for adults only. No Name/Date fields (removed in v2; student pages keep theirs). Explains fluency in plain language (accurate, smooth, expressive, with understanding, not speed). A simple 5-step routine per page (adult reads aloud first, child reads, child reads again, do the activity together, talk briefly about what happened). What to do when the child gets stuck on a word (wait, let them try, give the first sound, tell the word and move on). Support strategies if a story feels hard (read the tricky line together, break the story into two parts, point to each word, reread a favorite line first, stop while it is still fun). What NOT to do (no timer, no counting words per minute, no racing, no correcting every small slip). What to praise (smooth reading, expression, rereading, not speed). The five pages at a glance, one line each.
