# Handoff — Story Elements: Characters, Setting & Events (Kindergarten)

**Pack:** Story Elements — Characters, Setting & Events — 5 pages,
plus a one-page parent answer key
**Placement (for Codex):** Kindergarten → Reading & Language →
Literature / Comprehension
**Status:** Finalized and user-approved page by page, 2026-10-15.
NOT in directory.js; not merged; not published. Muse generated
the PDFs and this handoff; Codex handles review/integration as
usual.

Aligned to Michigan RL.K.3: with prompting and support, identify
characters, settings, and major events in a story. Every story is
short, original, and read aloud by an adult; all responses are
picture-based with minimal reading demand. The pack progresses:
characters → setting → a major event → all three on one detective
page → a story map that adds the main-character idea.

## Pages

1. **Who Is in the Story?** (characters) — Benny the bunny and Pip
   the bird share a carrot in a garden (new scene; the bunny choice
   is a purpose-made brown portrait matching Benny). Teaching
   strip: "Characters are who the story is about." Circle TWO:
   bunny (1st) and bird (3rd); distractors cat, fish.
2. **Where Does It Happen?** (setting) — Benny and Pip splash at
   the pond. Strip: "Setting is where the story happens." Circle
   the pond (middle) among forest / pond / desert habitat art; the
   pond choice is a different picture from the story scene, so the
   place must be recognized, not image-matched.
3. **What Happened?** (major event) — new character: Kit the
   kitten; a butterfly lands on her nose (the choice picture shows
   a small butterfly right on the nose tip, per the user's
   refinement). Strip: "Events are things that happen in a story."
   Distractors (milk, sleeping) never occur in the story — audited
   programmatically against the story text.
4. **Story Detective** — Max the puppy finds a big shell at the
   beach. Three rows, one correct each, positions 2nd/3rd/1st:
   WHO puppy · WHERE beach (new empty-beach picture) · WHAT Max
   discovering the shell (paws on it, sand flying — an action
   picture, per the user's refinement).
5. **My Story Map** — Molly the mouse finds a big cheese in a barn.
   Gold MY STORY MAP banner; three labeled sections. Deliberate
   step up: WHO has FOUR choices including the sleeping cat (in the
   story but minor — Molly is the subject of every sentence;
   correct 3rd of 4), WHERE the barn (1st), WHAT Molly eating the
   cheese (3rd); one distractor shows Molly sleeping, which never
   happens to her (only the cat sleeps).
