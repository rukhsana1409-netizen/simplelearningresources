## What this pack is

Four pages that teach a first grader to use the parts of a nonfiction book to find facts fast, and to tell whether a fact came from a picture or from the words. One coherent mini book, "All About the Seasons", runs across all pages. Contents: Spring page 2, Summer page 3, Fall page 4, Winter page 5. Facts: Spring "In spring, flowers bloom."; Summer "In summer, the days are long and hot."; Fall "In fall, leaves turn red, yellow, and orange."; Winter "In winter, snow falls." Glossary: bloom means a flower opens up; harvest means farmers pick food when it is ready; frost means a thin coat of ice on cold grass and leaves. Icons: magnifying glass means look closely; star means a big fact to remember; arrow means see that page.

## The four pages (as approved)

**Page 1. Meet the Text Features.** A sample book page from "All About the Seasons": Contents box (Spring 2, Summer 3, Fall 4, Winter 5), "Spring" heading, the sentence "In spring, the flowers **bloom**." with a magnifying glass icon, a small flower picture, "Page 2", and a small glossary entry box at the bottom ("Glossary / **bloom**: A flower opens up."). Below, four feature name cards (Table of Contents, Heading, Glossary Word, Icon) with teal start dots; the child draws a line from each card to the part it names. There is NO line from the bold word "bloom" to the glossary box: the child draws their own matching lines.

**Page 2. Find It Fast.** A Contents box (Spring 2, Summer 3, Fall 4, Winter 5) and four heading panels (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter), each with its sentence. Four questions, each with a write-in line: Which page tells about snow? (page 5), about flowers? (page 2), about leaves turning red? (page 4), about long, hot days? (page 3). The child uses the Contents, not memory.

**Page 3. Words to Know.** A glossary box on the left (bloom, harvest, frost with their meanings) and three sentences on the right, shuffled against the glossary order (harvest, frost, bloom), each with the glossary word in bold; the child draws a line from each bold word to what it means. Below, an Icons section: magnifying glass, star, and arrow, with their jobs shuffled (See that page, Look closely, A big fact to remember); the child draws a line from each picture to its job.

**Page 4. Picture or Words?** Six cells (3 rows x 2 columns), each with a vector-drawn scene, a sentence, a bold question, and three pills [Picture] [Words] [Both]. Answers, each unambiguous: 1. Picture (snowman with a red scarf; the words do not state the scarf color), 2. Words (fall leaves turn red, yellow, and orange; the tree shows red leaves only, so the picture alone does not give all three colors), 3. Both (flowers bloom; the picture shows a closed bud beside open flowers, and the words say "flowers bloom"), 4. Picture (beach ball on the sand under the sun; the words do not mention the ball), 5. Words (farmers harvest pumpkins; no farmer is drawn), 6. Both (snow falling on a house; the flakes are large with short downward motion dashes, and the words say "snow falls").

## The review corrections (her direction)

1. **Individual PDF quality (2026-11-18).** The individual page previews looked blurry compared with the combined PDF. The per-page PDFs are generated by splitting the final bundle losslessly (pypdf page copy), never from screenshots, thumbnails, or compressed previews. All four are pure vector (zero raster images), letter size, and each renders pixel-identical to its bundle page at 400 dpi. Preview PNGs are generated at 300 dpi for presentation.
2. **Page 1 glossary box (2026-11-18).** A small glossary entry box was added at the bottom of the sample page ("Glossary / bloom: A flower opens up.") so the child can see where the bold word is explained, without crowding the page.
3. **Page 1 connecting line removed (2026-11-18, final correction).** A thin teal line from the bold word "bloom" to the glossary box was drawn in the first revision and REMOVED at her direction, so children draw their own matching lines. The glossary box stays.
4. **Page 4 fall tree (2026-11-18).** The fall tree was changed from green leaves to red leaves only, so the picture does not contradict the sentence about red, yellow, and orange leaves. Yellow and orange appear only in the words. The answer stays Words.
5. **Page 4 flower picture (2026-11-18).** The flower picture now shows a closed pink bud beside two open flowers, so blooming is visible. The answer stays Both.
6. **Page 4 snow picture (2026-11-18).** The snowflakes were enlarged with short downward motion dashes under three flakes, so falling is clear. The answer stays Both.
7. **Standards corrected (2026-11-18, final correction).** RI.1.6 was listed in the first build and corrected to RI.1.7, which covers the Picture or Words activity on Page 4. RI.1.5 covers the text features on Pages 1 to 3.
8. **Parent Guide wording (2026-11-18, final correction, after approval).** In the Page 3 answers, "Glass means look closely" was changed to "Magnifying glass means look closely." The student pages are unchanged.
