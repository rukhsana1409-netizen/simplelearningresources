## Page order

1. Addition Within 10 — worked visual example (4 + 5 = 9),
   then 3+4, 5+5, 6+2, 4+6, 7+3, 2+8, 9+1, 5+4
2. Subtraction Within 10 — worked visual example (7 − 3 = 4),
   then 9−3, 7−7, 10−4, 6−2, 8−5, 10−10, 5−0, 9−6 (includes results of 0)
3. Fact Families Within 10 — 4 number bonds (10→6,4 · 9→3,6 · 8→2,6 · 10→5,5),
   each with one related addition and one related subtraction fact
4. Missing Numbers Within 10 — 6+□=10, 9−□=4, □+3=8, 10−□=7, □−4=2, 5+□=9, 8−□=8, □+5=10
5. Addition or Subtraction? — 4 short word problems (kites, cookies, cars, frogs);
   child writes the equation (number boxes + operator box + answer box) and solves
