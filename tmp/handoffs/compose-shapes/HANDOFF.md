## Page order

1. Put Shapes Together — example: 2 triangles → square; practice: draw the
   dividing line (2 triangles → square, 2 squares → rectangle,
   2 triangles → larger triangle)
2. Build New Shapes — example: 4 small squares → big square; practice:
   "How many small shapes make the big shape?" (4, 4, 2 incl. house)
3. Choose the Shapes — "Which set can make the big shape? Circle it."
   Correct sets show pieces SEPARATED by a small visible gap (never
   pre-assembled). Answers: B, A, B.
