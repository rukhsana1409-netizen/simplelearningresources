## What this pack is

Five pages that teach a first grader the words inside a sentence: the naming word, the action word, and the describing word. Page 1 asks the child to spot the naming word (person, place, or thing). Page 2 matches the action word to one or many. Page 3 places the action word in past, present, or future time. Page 4 adds describing words and whose words. Page 5 is independent writing about a beach picture, with a three-item self-check.

## The five pages (as approved)

**Page 1. Naming Words.** A person/place/thing visual reminder at the top (a girl reading, a red brick school, a big red ball, each labeled). Four watercolor rows (dog running, Mia reading, school, Ben kicking a ball). Each row has a sentence with a ruled blank and two word pills; the child circles the naming word that fits and writes it in the sentence. Correct answers: dog / Mia / school / Ben. Adult line below: "A name starts with a big letter. Ask: is it a person, a place, or a thing?"

**Page 2. One or Many?** Four watercolor rows (one boy hopping, three children hopping, one bird singing, two birds singing). Each row has a sentence with a ruled blank and two word pills; the child circles the action word that fits and writes it in the sentence. Correct answers: hops / hop / sings / sing. The second row uses "The children hop" with an illustration of multiple children (her direction). A Remember box at the bottom: "One hops. Many hop. / The action word changes when one or many do it." Adult line below: "Point to the picture and count. One child means hops. Many children means hop."

**Page 3. When Did It Happen?** A Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow visual strip at the top (past, now, yet to come), with arrows between the three boxes. Four watercolor rows (Ben kicking a ball, Mia playing with a ball, Sam with a bike at the park, dog running in the park). Each row shows the time word in the strip's color above the sentence, with a ruled blank and two word pills. Correct answers: kicked / plays / will play / played. A Remember box at the bottom: "Yesterday: played. Today: plays. Tomorrow: will play." Adult line below: "Say the time word first. Yesterday means it is done. Tomorrow is yet to come."

**Page 4. Tell Me More.** Two clearly distinct sections: "Describing words" (teal label and rule) and "Whose words" (coral label and rule). Describing rows: a very big red ball next to a tiny red ball (the big ball is obvious by comparison), and a yellow sun. Whose rows: Mia reading, and a dog holding a bone. Each row has a sentence with a ruled blank and two word pills. Correct answers: big / yellow / Mia's / dog's. A Remember box at the bottom: "Describing words tell what it is like: big, red, happy. / Whose words tell who it belongs to: Mia's, the dog's." Adult line below: "Ask: what is it like? Then ask: whose is it?"

**Page 5. Write Your Own Sentences.** One large watercolor beach scene shown whole (boy building a tall sandcastle, girl in a yellow dress holding a red bucket, brown dog running after a yellow ball, waves, big yellow sun, seashells). The instruction asks the child to write two sentences about the picture, using a naming word and an action word, with a describing word in at least one sentence. Two writing areas (Sentence 1, Sentence 2), each with two ruled lines. A self-check panel with three checkboxes: "Did I use a naming word?", "Did I use an action word?", "Did I use a describing word?" No adult line on this page. Page 5 is independent application of nouns, verbs, and adjectives, not assessment of all four standards.

## Builder and rebuild

**Builder:** `worksheet-generator/build_nouns_and_verbs_in_action.py`. It imports `draw_header` / `draw_footer` / `draw_logo` verbatim from `build_phonics_az.py`; no locked builder was modified. Shared design code is imported, never forked. The builder embeds the JPEG illustrations via a `_draw_image` helper (PIL aspect-preserving fit, `preserveAspectRatio=True`); JPEGs embed as DCT, so the PDF stays small. The builder reads the JPEGs from its output directory (`IMG_DIR = OUT_DIR`); to rebuild from a repo checkout, copy the 15 JPEGs into the output directory first.

**Rebuild command** (from a repo checkout, repo-local venv has reportlab, pypdf, and PIL):

```
cd worksheet-generator && ../.venv/bin/python build_nouns_and_verbs_in_action.py
```

This regenerates the 5-page bundle, the 1-page parent guide, the review PDF, and the five per-page PDFs (split losslessly from the bundle) into the output directory. Rebuilt files must match the hashes above byte-for-byte; the approved finals are the lock, do not re-render them for the website.
