## What this pack is

Five pages that teach a first grader to compare two short informational texts about the same topic. The child reads Book A and Book B about apples, finds what is the same and what is new, listens for the reason the author gives, works out a hard word from the words and picture near it, and tells what was learned in their own words. Pages 3, 4, and 5 each carry a readable reference panel with both book texts, so the child never has to answer from memory. Page 5 is independent practice with three generous drawing and writing spaces. Every answer is visible in the two texts or their pictures. No prediction, no inference beyond the text, no story-versus-information sorting.

## The two books (original texts, verbatim)

**Book A, How Apples Grow:** "Apples are a fruit. Apples grow on trees. Some apples are red. A flower grows first. Farmers pick the apples in fall because they are ripe."

**Book B, Eating Apples:** "Apples are a fruit. Apples are good to eat. Some apples are red. We can cut an apple into slices. We drink apple juice because it tastes sweet."

Shared facts: Apples are a fruit; Some apples are red. Only in Book A: Apples grow on trees; A flower grows first; Farmers pick the apples in fall because they are ripe. Only in Book B: Apples are good to eat; We can cut an apple into slices; We drink apple juice because it tastes sweet. The author's reasons: farmers pick the apples because they are ripe (Book A); we drink apple juice because it tastes sweet (Book B). Guided words (RI.1.4): ripe (P1), sweet (P2).

## The five pages (as approved)

**Page 1. Meet Book A.** "Read Book A. Then answer the questions." Book A beside a watercolor apple tree with red apples and white blossoms. Questions: "What is this book about? Circle one. apples / trees" (answer: apples); "Where do apples grow? Circle one. trees / flowers" (answer: trees); "What does ripe mean? Circle one. ready to pick / green and hard" (answer: ready to pick). A Remember box: "Apples grow on trees. / Farmers pick the apples in fall because they are ripe." Adult line below: "Point to the apple tree. Ask: Where do apples grow?"

**Page 2. Meet Book B.** "Read Book B. Then answer the questions." Book B beside a watercolor of apple slices on a plate with a glass of apple juice. Questions: "What is this book about? Circle one. apples / juice" (answer: apples); "What can we drink? Circle one. apple juice / milk" (answer: apple juice); "What does sweet mean? Circle one. tastes sugary / tastes sour" (answer: tastes sugary). A Remember box: "Apples are good to eat. / We drink apple juice because it tastes sweet." Adult line below: "Point to the apple slices. Ask: What can we drink?"

**Page 3. Same and Different.** "Read both books. For each fact, circle where it goes." A two-column reference panel with both book texts at the top. Six facts, each with Both Books / Only A / Only B pills: "Apples are a fruit." (Both Books); "Some apples are red." (Both Books); "Apples grow on trees." (Only A); "Farmers pick the apples in fall because they are ripe." (Only A, full original wording, wrapped onto two lines); "Apples are good to eat." (Only B); "We drink apple juice because it tastes sweet." (Only B, full original wording, wrapped onto two lines). A Remember box: "Two books can tell about one topic. / Some facts are the same. Some facts are new." A small watercolor of two books on a shelf (tree cover and slices/juice cover, no words on the covers). Adult line below: "Ask: Is this fact in both books, or only in one?"

**Page 4. Why Does the Author Say So?** "Read both books. Match each point to its reason." A two-column reference panel with both book texts at the top. Match rows with pictures: a watercolor farmer in a straw hat picking red apples into a basket, "Farmers pick the apples because" with choices "they are ripe" / "it tastes sweet" (answer: they are ripe); the apple slices and juice picture, "We drink apple juice because" with choices "they are ripe" / "it tastes sweet" (answer: it tastes sweet). Then two source questions: "Which book tells why farmers pick the apples?" (answer: Book A) and "Which book tells why we drink apple juice?" (answer: Book B). A Remember box: "Farmers pick the apples because they are ripe. / We drink apple juice because it tastes sweet." Adult line below: "Ask: Why do farmers pick the apples? The book says they are ripe."

**Page 5. My Two-Book Page.** "Use both books. Draw or write three facts." A two-column reference panel with both book texts at the top. Three generous response boxes, each 100pt tall with open drawing room above and exactly one writing line at the bottom: "One fact only in Book A", "One fact only in Book B", "The same in both books". A "Check your work" self-check with three tick boxes: "I used a fact from Book A. / I used a fact from Book B. / I told what is the same." No adult line on this page. Page 5 is independent practice, not assessment.
