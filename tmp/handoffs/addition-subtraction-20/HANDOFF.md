## Page order

1. Addition Within 20 — worked visual example (9 + 6 = 15),
   then 8+5, 12+3, 9+7, 6+9, 14+4, 11+8, 7+6, 13+5
2. Subtraction Within 20 — worked visual example (12 − 5 = 7),
   then 14−6, 18−9, 12−4, 20−5, 16−8, 11−3, 15−15, 13−7 (includes result of 0)
3. Fact Families Within 20 — 4 number bonds (15→9,6 · 18→10,8 · 13→7,6 · 20→12,8),
   each with one related addition and one related subtraction fact
4. Missing Numbers Within 20 — 8+□=13, 16−□=9, □+7=15, 20−□=12, □−5=11, 9+□=17, 14−□=14, □+9=18
5. Addition or Subtraction? — 4 short word problems (birds, balloons, frogs, fish);
   child writes the equation (number boxes + operator box + answer box) and solves
