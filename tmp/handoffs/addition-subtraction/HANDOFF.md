## Page order

1. Addition Within 5 — worked visual example (2 dots + 3 dots → 2 + 3 = 5),
   then 8 numeral equations: 1+2, 2+2, 3+1, 2+3, 4+1, 0+4, 1+4, 3+2
2. Subtraction Within 5 — worked visual example (5 dots, 2 crossed out → 5 − 2 = 3),
   then 5−2, 4−1, 3−3, 5−4, 2−0, 4−3, 5−1, 3−1
3. Fact Families Within 5 — 4 number bonds (5→2,3 · 4→1,3 · 5→1,4 · 3→1,2),
   each with one related addition and one related subtraction fact
4. Missing Numbers Within 5 — 2+□=5, 5−□=3, □+1=4, 4−□=2, □−2=1, 3+□=4, 5−□=0, □+2=5
5. Addition or Subtraction? — 4 short word problems (birds, fish, apples, ducks);
   child writes the equation (number boxes + operator box + answer box) and solves
