## What this pack is

Five pages that teach 12 common contractions by showing how two words join into one short word, with the apostrophe taking the place of the missing letters. The apostrophe is drawn in teal on Pages 1-2 so the child sees it. No whole-word memorization, no phonics terminology on the child pages. The 12 contractions are split into two sets of six: Set 1 don't, can't, isn't, didn't, won't, it's; Set 2 I'm, that's, we're, you're, I'll, I've.

## The five pages (as approved)

**Page 1. Meet the Contractions (Set 1).** Six panels, each with the contraction large (apostrophe in teal), the two words it comes from, a picture, and a short phrase. don't (do + not, "I don't know."), can't (can + not, "I can't reach it."), isn't (is + not, "It isn't mine."), didn't (did + not, "I didn't see it."), won't (will + not, "It won't fit."), it's (it + is, "It's sunny.").

**Page 2. Meet More Contractions (Set 2).** Six panels, same layout. I'm (I + am, "I'm happy."), that's (that + is, "That's my dog."), we're (we + are, "We're friends."), you're (you + are, "You're my friend."), I'll (I + will, "I'll help you."), I've (I + have, "I've got a red ball.").

**Page 3. Build the Contraction (Set 1).** Six spacious activities, one per Set 1 word. Each panel shows the two words with a plus sign, the word "makes", a picture, and a ruled line where the child writes the contraction including the apostrophe. Answers: don't, can't, isn't, didn't, won't, it's. Each panel has the matching picture from Page 1. Set 2 gets its building practice on Page 4 (writing the word in a sentence) and Page 5 (writing sentences of the child's own).

**Page 4. Use the Contractions in Sentences (Set 2).** A shuffled word bank (you're, I've, I'll, that's, we're, I'm) and six sentences, each with a picture at the right. Exactly one word fits each sentence, verified: 1. "___ all by myself." (I'm; "myself" is first-person singular, so only "I am" can take it), 2. "___ not my dog." (that's; "not" blocks "I've", since "I have not my dog" is not standard English), 3. "___ going to the park together." (we're; the picture shows the two children speaking about themselves as they go together), 4. "___ my best friend." (you're; the picture shows direct face-to-face address, and "that's" is already used in sentence 2), 5. "___ help you." (I'll; only "I will" takes the base verb "help"), 6. "___ been to the lake." (I've; only "I have" takes the past participle "been").

**Page 5. Read and Write on Your Own.** A short, natural picnic story (53 words, 7 sentences) about Ben and Mia getting ready for a picnic. It uses seven contractions where they fit naturally (I'm, it's, I'll, I've, we're, that's, you're); the other five (don't, can't, isn't, didn't, won't) are not forced in. Dialogue is carried by action beats (Mia smiled, Ben took, Mia held up, Ben took, Mia was glad, Ben smiled) with only one "said", so it reads naturally. Story: "Ben and Mia are getting ready for a picnic. 'I'm so glad it's sunny!' Mia smiled. 'I'll pack the apples,' said Ben. 'I've got the cups!' Mia held up the cups. 'We're going to the park.' Ben took the basket. 'That's a good plan!' Mia was glad. 'You're the best helper!' Ben smiled." Picture: two children with a picnic basket. Below the story, a 12-word bank and two spacious writing areas (numbered, two ruled lines each) for two sentences of the child's own. The instruction requires a contraction in each sentence.

## The review corrections (her direction)

1. **v1 to v2 (Page 3 overlap, Page 4 sentences, Page 5 story).** Page 3: the first row of panels overlapped the two-line instruction (a hardcoded position did not account for the two-line instruction), so the rows were moved down to start at the content top. The ">" arrow between the two words and the writing line was changed to the word "makes", since ">" is also the math "greater than" symbol. Page 4: all six sentences were rewritten so each has exactly one fitting contraction, with context added where it was ambiguous: "all by myself" for I'm (only "I am" can take "myself"), "not my dog" for that's (the "not" blocks "I've"), "together, Ben and I" for we're (the appositive names who "we" is). Page 5: the picnic story was smoothed with action beats instead of repeating "said Mia" and "said Ben", and the writing instruction was changed to require a contraction in each sentence. Pages 1 and 2 unchanged.
2. **v2 to v3 (Page 4 sentence 3 only).** Sentence 3 changed from "___ together, Ben and I." to "___ going to the park together." The answer is still we're. The word bank, the other five sentences, the illustrations, the layout, and the spacing are unchanged. Pages 1, 2, 3, and 5 unchanged.

## Parent Guide (separate one-page guide, NOT in the student bundle)

One page, for adults only. Plain language, no standards codes, no em dash. Gives the helping routine (say the two words, then the short word, point to the apostrophe), the two words and missing letters in each of the 12 contractions with a clear note that won't is irregular (will changes to wo, and the o in not is missing; it does not follow the usual rule), all answers (Page 3 contractions, Page 4 sentences, Page 5 story words), and a short "If your child finds it hard" section.

## Builder and rebuild

**Builder:** `worksheet-generator/build_contractions.py`. It imports `draw_header` / `draw_footer` / `_img_circle` / `draw_logo` verbatim from `build_phonics_az.py`; no locked builder was modified. Shared design code is imported, never forked. A new helper draws the contraction with the apostrophe in teal (the `_marked_word` dot/line markings are NOT used in this pack). Note: the build runs from the `simplelearningresources` clone because `build_phonics_az.py` (the shared header/logo source) lives only there.

**Rebuild command** (from a repo checkout, repo-local venv has reportlab):

```
cd worksheet-generator && ../.venv/bin/python build_contractions.py
```

This regenerates the per-page PDFs, the 5-page bundle, and the 1-page parent guide into `~/workspace/your_files/contractions/`. Rebuilt files must match the hashes above byte-for-byte; the approved finals are the lock, do not re-render them for the website.
