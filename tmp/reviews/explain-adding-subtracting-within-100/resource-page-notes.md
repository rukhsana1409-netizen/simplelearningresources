## Why this pack exists

Adding Tens and Ones and Subtracting Tens and Ones show the
tens-and-ones models and the computation, but no page asks a child
to explain a method in words. This pack closes that
explain-reasoning gap. It does NOT repeat computation practice:
the only numbers a child writes are the tens, ones, and totals
inside the explanation sentences themselves, plus circling the
true explanation. No regrouping anywhere: every ones digit stays
within its own place, tens with tens, ones with ones.

## Page 1: Explain How You Add

Instruction: "Say how you add. Finish the sentences."

Worked example, 35 + 42 = 77, with base-ten models for 35, 42,
and the sum 77:
"I added the tens first. 3 tens and 4 tens make 7 tens.
Then I added the ones. 5 ones and 2 ones make 7 ones.
So 35 + 42 = 77."

A. Finish the sentences, 61 + 24, models for 61 and 24:
"I added the tens first. 6 tens and 2 tens make 8 tens.
Then I added the ones. 1 one and 4 ones make 5 ones.
So 61 + 24 = 85."

B. Circle the true explanation for 53 + 35 = 88.
TRUE (first box): "I added the tens first. 5 tens and 3 tens
make 8 tens. Then I added the ones. 3 ones and 5 ones make
8 ones. So 53 + 35 = 88."
FALSE (second box): same, but "5 tens and 3 tens make 8 ones"
and "3 ones and 5 ones make 8 tens". The error is the swapped
tens/ones labels, not the total.

## Page 2: Explain How You Subtract

Instruction: "Say how you subtract. Finish the sentences."

Worked example, 79 - 36 = 43, with the 79 model crossed out
(3 tens and 6 ones in coral) and an arrow to the 43 model:
"I subtracted the tens first. 7 tens take away 3 tens is
4 tens. Then I subtracted the ones. 9 ones take away 6 ones
is 3 ones. So 79 - 36 = 43."

A. Finish the sentences, 65 - 41, model for 65 with 4 tens
and 1 one crossed out:
"I subtracted the tens first. 6 tens take away 4 tens is
2 tens. Then I subtracted the ones. 5 ones take away 1 one
is 4 ones. So 65 - 41 = 24."

B. Circle the true explanation for 88 - 52 = 36.
TRUE (first box): "I subtracted the tens first. 8 tens take
away 5 tens is 3 tens. Then I subtracted the ones. 8 ones
take away 2 ones is 6 ones. So 88 - 52 = 36."
FALSE (second box): "I subtracted the ones first. 8 ones take
away 2 ones is 6 tens. Then I subtracted the tens. 8 tens
take away 5 tens is 3 ones. So 88 - 52 = 36." The error is
both the order and the swapped tens/ones labels.
