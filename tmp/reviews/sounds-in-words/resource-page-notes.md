## How to use this pack

This pack is oral-first. The adult says the words aloud and the child
listens, says, points, or circles. There are no printed target words
for the child anywhere on the pages, and no letters, spelling, or
decoding. That keeps phonemic awareness (hearing sounds) distinct from
printed phonics. Each page prints its "Say:" prompts in sound notation
for the adult, plus one short "Adult:" guidance line. All sounds are
General American English, counted as sounds (phonemes), not letters.

## Page 1: Long or Short? (RF.1.2a)

Example first, adult-led and oral: say "name". The a says its name, so
it is LONG. Say "map". The a is short, so it is SHORT. The example
uses name/map so it does not reveal the answers to the first two
practice questions (cake, cat). Then the 8 questions, in order: cake
(LONG), cat (SHORT), kite (LONG), kit (SHORT), rope (LONG), rock
(SHORT), tube (LONG), tub (SHORT). The adult says each word once,
clearly, without stretching the vowel or naming the letter.

## Page 2: Blend the Sounds (RF.1.2b)

The adult says the sounds one at a time with a short pause; the child
blends them and circles the matching picture. Answers, in order:
1. /s/ /t/ /o/ /p/ is stop (near-miss: top)
2. /f/ /l/ /a/ /g/ is flag (near-miss: bag; same rime, different onset)
3. /s/ /n/ /i/ /p/ is snip (near-miss: sip)
4. /b/ /r/ /i/ /dj/ is bridge (near-miss: fridge; one sound differs)
5. /k/ /l/ /a/ /p/ is clap (near-miss: cap)
6. /s/ /t/ /a/ /n/ /d/ is stand (near-miss: sand)

## Page 3: Find the Sounds: Beginning, Vowel, Ending. (RF.1.2c)

The adult says the word once and asks for the first sound, the middle
vowel sound, and the last sound. The child says the sounds and the
adult writes what the child says in the three boxes. The boxes are
40pt, large enough for an adult to write phoneme notation. The boxes
hold three selected sounds only, not every sound in the word. Answers,
in order:
1. flag: /f/, /a/, /g/
2. stop: /s/, /o/, /p/
3. milk: /m/, /i/, /k/
4. nest: /n/, /e/, /t/
5. clip: /k/, /i/, /p/
6. clap: /k/, /a/, /p/

## Page 4: Say Every Sound (RF.1.2d)

Dots only: the adult says the word once. The child says each sound
slowly, in order, and draws one dot in each sound box. The adult does
not write phonemes in these boxes. The sounds in order below are for
the adult to check against, in these notes only:
1. clap: /k/ /l/ /a/ /p/ (4 sounds)
2. tent: /t/ /e/ /n/ /t/ (4 sounds)
3. milk: /m/ /i/ /l/ /k/ (4 sounds)
4. stamp: /s/ /t/ /a/ /m/ /p/ (5 sounds)
5. stand: /s/ /t/ /a/ /n/ /d/ (5 sounds)
6. blimp: /b/ /l/ /i/ /m/ /p/ (5 sounds)

## What is new against Kindergarten

Kindergarten Blending and Segmenting works only short vowels, only CVC
words, blends of 2 to 3 sounds, onset-rime, first and last sounds only,
and counts sounds in boxes at 3-sound level. Kindergarten Say It and
Split It works rhymes, syllables, and splitting the first sound from
the rest. This pack adds long vowels by ear, consonant blends, 4 to 5
sound words, middle vowel isolation, and full segmentation in order.
No activity repeats a Kindergarten one.
