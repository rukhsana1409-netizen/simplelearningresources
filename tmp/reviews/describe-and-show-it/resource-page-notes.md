## How to use this pack

This pack helps a child describe a person, place, thing, or event
with relevant details in complete sentences, then draw it to help
explain their ideas. On each page, ask the child to describe it out
loud first, then write and draw. The sentence frames fade across the
pack: full frames on page 1, shorter frames on page 2, complete
starters on page 3, and open lines on page 4, so the child builds
toward writing their own describing sentences. One page a day works
well.

Page 1 describes a thing the child loves. Page 2 describes a place
the child loves. Page 3 describes a special person. Page 4 is an
independent show and tell: the child chooses their own person,
place, thing, or event, draws it, writes about it, and shows it to
someone, telling them three things about it.

## Standards (resource page only, never on the child pages)

SL.1.4: describe a person, place, thing, or event with relevant
details in complete sentences. SL.1.5: add drawings to clarify ideas.

## For adults

Let your child pick a familiar person, place, thing, or event. On
each page, ask them to describe it out loud first, then write and
draw. The sentence frames fade across the pack: full frames on page
1, shorter frames on page 2, complete starters on page 3, and open
lines on page 4, so your child builds toward writing their own
describing sentences. This pack builds two Grade 1 speaking and
listening goals: describing a person, place, thing, or event with
relevant details in complete sentences (SL.1.4), and adding drawings
to clarify ideas (SL.1.5). On page 4, encourage your child to show
their drawing to someone and tell them three things about it.

## Page 1: Describe a Thing

The child describes a thing they love out loud, then writes two
complete describing sentences using the full sentence frames, and
draws the thing.

## Page 2: Describe a Place

The child draws a place they love, then writes what they can see and
hear there, using the 'I chose' frame.

## Page 3: Describe a Person

The child describes a special person, how they look and how they
make them feel, using the complete left-aligned starters, and draws
them.

## Page 4: My Show and Tell

The child chooses their own person, place, thing, or event, draws
it, writes about it, and shows it to someone, telling them three
things about it, with the Show and Tell reminder.
