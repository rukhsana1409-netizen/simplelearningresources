## How to use this pack

This pack helps a child plan, tell, write, and revise one small
moment from real life, a moment that happened to them. Let the
child pick the moment. A small moment works best: a walk, a snack,
a game, a visit, not a whole day. One page a day works well.

Page 1 plans the moment in three boxes: Beginning, Middle, End.
Page 2 tells it in order with First, Next, Last. Page 3 adds a
detail: who was there, where it happened, and how the child felt.
Page 4 is independent writing: the child writes the story on open
lines and draws a picture. Page 5 revises: the child rereads the
story, picks one place to add a detail, marks it with a star, and
writes the detail in.

## Standards (resource page only, never on the child pages)

W.1.3: write a narrative with events in order, details, time
words, and a closing. W.1.5: focus on a topic and add details to
strengthen writing.

## For adults

Let the child pick one real small moment. Read the story together.
Help them add one detail. Celebrate the writing. If the child
cannot think of a moment, offer two of your own small moments and
let them choose one. Do not write the story for the child; the
child's own words, even short ones, are the goal.

## Page 1: Plan Your Small Moment

The child names the moment and draws it in order: Beginning,
Middle, End. The picture on this page is a spark to get them
thinking.

## Page 2: Tell It in Order

Three sections, First, Next, Last, matching Page 1. Three
handwriting rows in each section. The child tells the moment in
order, one part at a time.

## Page 3: Add a Detail

A feeling-words bank (happy, excited, proud, surprised, calm,
silly) with faces, and three prompts: Who was with you? Where
were you? How did you feel? The child picks and writes.

## Page 4: Write Your Story

Independent writing, no sentence frames. An illustration box at
the top and six open ruled lines below. The child writes the whole
story here.

## Page 5: Make It Better

A short checklist, then Add one more detail, then the Revise your
story panel with three numbered steps: reread the story on Page 4,
choose one place to add a detail and draw a star on Page 4, write
the detail into the story on Page 4.
