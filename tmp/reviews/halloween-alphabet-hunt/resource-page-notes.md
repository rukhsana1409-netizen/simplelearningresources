# Halloween Alphabet Hunt Pack (Preschool & Kindergarten)

A cheerful Halloween alphabet pack for preschool and kindergarten.
Six printable pages, all friendly and not scary, with big letters,
large response areas, and short instructions.

## What children learn

- Recognize all 26 uppercase letters, A to Z.
- Hear the first sound in a Halloween word and match the picture to
  its beginning letter.
- Match big (uppercase) letters to little (lowercase) letters.
- Tell look-alike letters apart.
- Name the letter that comes next in alphabet order.
- Sort big and little letters by coloring them.

## The six pages

1. Halloween ABC Matching: draw a line from each Halloween picture
   (bat, pumpkin, ghost, cat) to its beginning letter.
2. Halloween Alphabet Hunt: find and circle all 26 uppercase letters
   in a mixed-up grid, then check each one off on the A to Z checklist.
3. Big and Little Match: draw a line from each big letter to its
   little letter.
4. Find the Same Letter: circle every letter that matches the first
   letter in the row.
5. What Comes Next?: circle the letter that comes next in the sequence.
6. Color the Letters: color the big letters orange and the little
   letters purple.

## Parent guidance

- Print one page at a time. A pencil works for matching, circling, and
  checking off; crayons work for the coloring page.
- Let your child try first, then check together. For the alphabet hunt,
  checking off the list together is part of the fun.
- Say the letter names aloud as you go. For the big and little pages,
  name both forms, for example "big B, little b."
- There is no parent answer sheet with this pack, by design. The
  activities are short and self-checking: each page has one clear task.
