## What is inside

- Page 1, Count from 0: by 1s 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5; by 2s 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10;
  by 5s 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25; by 10s 0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, then
  keep going 60, 70, 80, 90, 100. The by 1s, 2s, and 5s rows are
  6-step starters from 0, not full runs to 100, by approved plan.
- Page 2, Words and Numerals: A, draw a line, twelve to 12,
  eighteen to 18, twenty to 20, thirty to 30, fifty to 50,
  ninety to 90. B, circle the word, 14 fourteen, 70 seventy,
  19 nineteen, 40 forty. No child writes a number word from
  memory; words are always in a bank to match or circle.
- Page 3, Tens and Ones in Words: A worked, 1 ten and 5 ones
  is 15; practice 14, 18, 20, 70, 90. B worked, 30 is 3 tens
  and 0 ones; practice 12 is 1 ten and 2 ones, 16 is 1 ten
  and 6 ones, 60 is 6 tens and 0 ones, 80 is 8 tens and 0 ones.
  Words only, no rods, no drawing rods.
- Page 4, Count the Objects: worked 12 dots; 1, 14 dots;
  2, 27 dots; 3, 34 dots (3 groups of 10 plus 4 singles);
  4, 52 dots (5 groups of 10 plus 2 singles); 5, 80 dots
  (8 groups of 10). Dots only, one consistent object type in
  every question. Groups of ten are separated by spacing and
  by a teal, gold, coral colour cycle, with no rods, no grids,
  and no ten-frames. All 80 dots are individually countable.

## Supplementary learning guidance (resource page only, not on child pages)

- Placement, for Codex: Grade 1 Math, numbers-and-place-value
  folder, as a full pack, not a supplement. Use after Counting
  to 120, Counting Patterns, and Tens and Ones.
- What is new here: counting from 0 itself, not from a given
  start or a non-multiple start; number words for 0 to 100,
  with a teen versus ten check; tens-and-ones descriptions in
  words, without rods; and loose-object counting, where the
  numeral represents a counted group of objects.
- Page 1 note: the point is starting at 0 and the size of the
  step, not reaching 100 on every track. The to-100 run is the
  by-10s track, and the to-100 numbers themselves are in
  Pages 2 to 4.
- Page 4 note: colour is a group separator only. The child
  counts every dot, whatever its colour, and the singles in
  34 and 52 are in navy so the ones stand apart from the tens.
