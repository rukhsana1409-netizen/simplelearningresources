## What is inside

- Page 1, Group Differently, Same Total: worked 2 + 6 + 4,
  (2 + 6) + 4 = 12 and 2 + (6 + 4) = 2 + 10 = 12, Same total.
  Practice: (5 + 7) + 3 = 15 and 5 + (7 + 3) = 5 + 10 = 15;
  (3 + 4) + 5 = 12 and 3 + (4 + 5) = 3 + 9 = 12;
  (4 + 3) + 6 = 13 and 4 + (3 + 6) = 4 + 9 = 13.
  Both groupings are printed on every row, including 4 + 3 + 6,
  per the 2026-11-04 clarification. The child writes both totals
  and circles Same or Different.
- Page 2, One Fact, Two Ways: Family 15, guided, 6 + 9 = 15,
  9 + 6 = 15, 15 - 9 = 6, 15 - 6 = 9; Family 17, independent,
  9 + 8 = 17, 8 + 9 = 17, 17 - 9 = 8, 17 - 8 = 9.
  Think addition: 9 + 7 = 16, so 16 - 9 = 7;
  7 + 8 = 15, so 15 - 7 = 8.

## Supplementary learning guidance (resource page only, not on child pages)

- Placement: supplementary practice, use after Addition Fact
  Strategies, Subtraction Fact Strategies, and Unknown Numbers.
  Not a first introduction. Proposed website placement, for Codex:
  Grade 1 Math, addition folder, as a single 2-page supplementary
  resource card, cross-referenced to those three packs.
- Page 1 teaching point: changing the grouping does not change
  the total. Make 10 is only one useful grouping, not the lesson:
  3 + 4 + 5 has no make-10 grouping and still gives 12 both ways.
  The dot groups in the worked example use three colours, teal,
  gold, and coral, so the three numbers stay visible as groups.
- Page 2 teaching point: one family makes four facts, including
  the swapped addition that the earlier Subtraction Strategies
  families did not show. In Section 2, the addition partner is
  written first, then the subtraction answer, so addition is the
  method, not an afterthought. The 15 in 15 - 7 is a separate
  problem from the 6 + 9 family above, not a claim that it comes
  from that family.
- What this supplement does not re-teach: count-on, make-10
  first as a strategy lesson, count-up, count-back, number lines,
  rods, or word problems. Those stay in their own packs, and no
  earlier pack PDF is altered by this supplement.
