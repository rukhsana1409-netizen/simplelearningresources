# Read and Think: Understanding Stories

## Resource details

- **Pack:** Read and Think: Understanding Stories
- **Level:** Grade 1 Reading, Reading Comprehension
- **Subtitle:** Grade 1 Reading | Reading Comprehension
- **Standards:** RL.1.1 (ask and answer questions about key details in a story), RL.1.2 (retell a story and say what it teaches), RL.1.3 (say who is in the story, where it happens, what happens, and why a character acts), RL.1.7 (use pictures plus story details to describe the story)
- **Pages:** 5, US Letter
- **Status:** DRAFT for visual review. Plan approved 2026-11-09 with final corrections (P3 "After many days", P4 rebuild with a small block, P5 Dad reaches the balloon and "cannot"). Not locked, not handed off, not merged to main, not published.
- **Answer key:** None printed. Answers are in these notes only.

## How it differs from the Kindergarten pack

The original Read, Look and Retell pack was reclassified to Kindergarten. This is a separate, more challenging pack with new stories and new characters. The stories use familiar Grade 1 vocabulary and high-frequency words rather than staying tightly inside the phonics scope, so they read as real stories. The work goes further: key details that need tracking, character motivation, a connected cause-and-effect chain, sequencing where two similar events must be told apart by reading, a text-grounded inference, and an independent retelling. Difficulty runs from supported (word bank) on Page 1 to independent (no starters) on Page 5. No prediction. Every inference is grounded in the text.

## Page-by-page answers

### Page 1. Key Details (RL.1.1)
Story: "Nina has a big red wagon. On Saturday, Nina puts her toys in the wagon. She pulls the wagon to the park. At the park, Nina sees her friend Leo. Leo has a blue ball. Nina and Leo play with the ball. Then Nina puts the ball in the wagon. Nina and Leo pull the wagon home."
Word bank: Saturday, Sunday, her toys, the blue ball, Leo, Mom, her lunch, the red wagon.
- What day does Nina go to the park? Saturday.
- What does Nina put in the wagon first? Her toys.
- Who does Nina see at the park? Leo.
- What do Nina and Leo play with? The blue ball.
The "first" question is the key one: the child must track that the toys go in before the ball.

### Page 2. Why Does Milo Help? (RL.1.3, RL.1.1)
Story: "Milo sees a little puppy in the park. The puppy is all alone. Milo takes the puppy home. He gives the puppy food and water. Milo puts up a sign that says, 'Lost puppy.' The next day, a girl comes to the door. 'That is my puppy!' she says. Milo is glad the puppy found its home."
- Why does Milo take the puppy home? "Milo takes the puppy home because it is all alone." Accept: it has no home, it is lost.
- What does Milo do to help the puppy find its home? "Milo puts up a sign."
- How does Milo feel at the end? Glad (circle). "Milo is glad because the puppy found its home."

### Page 3. What Makes It Happen? (RL.1.3, RL.1.1)
Story: "Ruby plants seeds in her garden. The sun is hot, and the soil gets dry. Ruby brings water from the house. She gives the plants a big drink. After many days, the plants grow tall and strong. Ruby picks red flowers. She gives the flowers to her mom."
Matching: "The sun is hot." goes to "The soil gets dry." "Ruby gives the plants water." goes to "The plants grow tall and strong." "The plants grow tall." goes to "Ruby picks red flowers." The chain is connected: each step causes the next.
- Why does Ruby give the plants water? "Ruby gives the plants water because the soil is dry." Accept: the sun is hot and the soil is dry.

### Page 4. Put the Story in Order (RL.1.3, RL.1.7)
The four panels are printed in mixed order; the child reads each part and writes 1, 2, 3, or 4.
- "Oscar builds a tall tower." is 1 (beginning).
- "He puts a big block on top." is 2.
- "The tower falls down." is 3 (problem).
- "Oscar builds the tower again with a small block on top. The tower stands tall." is 4 (resolution).
The two "puts a block on top" panels look alike, so the child must read the size words and the outcomes to order them: the big block makes the tower fall, the small block is the fix.

### Page 5. Retell and Think (RL.1.2, RL.1.1)
Story: "Ivy has a big red balloon. She takes it to the park. The wind blows hard. The balloon flies up into a tree. Ivy cannot reach it. She runs to get her dad. Dad reaches up high and gets the balloon. He gives it back to Ivy. Ivy holds the balloon tight."
- Why does Ivy run to get her dad? She cannot reach the balloon. Accept: it is too high for her.
- Retell: Beginning (Ivy takes her balloon to the park), Middle (the wind blows it into a tree, she cannot reach it, she runs for Dad), End (Dad reaches up, gets the balloon, gives it back, Ivy holds it tight). Word bank for spelling support: balloon, park, wind, tree, Dad. No sentence starters. Accept any retelling that keeps the three parts in order with the key details.

## Parent guidance

The stories are for reading, not just looking: the child reads the words, the pictures help. If stuck on a word, read the rest of the sentence, then come back to it. On Page 2 and Page 5, ask "why" out loud before the child writes: the reason is always in the story. One page a day; reread the Page 5 story together before the retell.
