## What the child does

Cut out 4 triangles and 2 squares from the cut-out area, then paste
them together in the work area to build two bigger shapes: a square
from the 4 triangles, and a rectangle from the 2 squares. Then finish
four sentences using sides and corners language.

## Answers (internal only, not on the page)

- Build 1: 4 triangles make a square. The square has 4 sides and
  4 corners.
- Build 2: 2 squares make a rectangle. The rectangle has 4 sides
  and 4 corners.
- A triangle has 3 sides and 3 corners (background vocabulary).

## Geometry (exact fit, verified at vector level in the locked PDF)

- Big square outline: 160 by 160 pt. Each triangle has base 160 pt
  and height 80 pt, apex at the square's centre. Four identical
  triangles fill the square exactly: 4 x (160 x 80 / 2) = 160 x 160.
  No gaps, no overlaps. Bases form the four outside edges; tips
  meet at the centre. Placement guides are light dashed diagonals
  only.
- Big rectangle outline: 160 by 80 pt, made of two 80 by 80 pt
  squares, with a light dashed midline as the placement guide.
- Cut-out squares measured on the render at actual print size
  (72 dpi, 1 px = 1 pt): 81 x 81 pt including the stroke, true
  squares, each exactly half the target rectangle.
- Revision 2026-11-05: the squares were already exact, but stacked
  with only a 10 pt gap they read as one tall column. They now sit
  18 pt apart, centred in the cut area, so each reads as its own
  square. Triangles, work area, text, answer boxes, colours, and
  overall layout unchanged by that revision.

## What makes it new

- Kindergarten Compose Shapes is choose-the-pieces only.
- Shapes and Their Attributes identifies and describes shapes but
  never asks the child to build one.
- This page is hands-on cut-and-paste composing with exact-fit
  pieces.
