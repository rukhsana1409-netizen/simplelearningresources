## How to use this pack

This pack helps a child state an opinion about something they know,
give a specific reason using "because," and write a short review with
a closing sentence. Let the child pick a topic they care about: a book,
a game, a food, or an activity. One page a day works well.

## Resource page notes

Learning objective: the child states an opinion about a familiar topic,
gives a specific reason using "because," and writes a short review with
a closing sentence.

For adults: let your child pick a topic they care about. Help them say
their opinion in one sentence, then ask "Why?" to draw out a specific
reason. Read the finished review together and celebrate the closing
line. This pack builds the Grade 1 writing goal of writing an opinion
piece with an opinion, a reason, and a closing (W.1.1). If your child
gets stuck, offer two topics and let them choose one.

Page 2 note: encourage specific reasons, not vague ones. A good reason
says why: it has funny pictures, it tastes sweet and crunchy, it is fun
to play with friends. "Because it is good" does not tell why.
