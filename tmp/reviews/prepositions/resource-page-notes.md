# Prepositions: Parent Notes (grown-ups only)

A single Grade 1 sheet: five pictures, three word choices beside each
picture, the child circles the right preposition.

## Learning objective

The child recognizes frequently occurring prepositions from pictures
and picks the word that matches: under, above, near, far, between.
Grade 1 standard L.1.1(i): use frequently occurring prepositions.
Website placement: Grade 1, Reading and Language, Grammar and
Sentences. This sheet covers recognition only; using prepositions in
the child's own speech is practiced in the Say It in Real Life pack.

## How to use it

Let the child look at each picture first and say what they see, then
read the three choices and circle the one that matches. If they are
stuck, describe the picture back without naming the answer, for
example "Where is the cat, compared to the table?"

## Answers (top to bottom)

1. under: the cat is under the table.
2. above: the kite is above the tree.
3. near: the red ball is close up on the path.
4. far: the red ball is tiny at the end of the path.
5. between: the red ball is between the two blue boxes.
