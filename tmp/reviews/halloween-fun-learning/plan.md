# Halloween Fun & Learning (Preschool & Kindergarten) — DRAFT PLAN

Status: DRAFT. Page 1 and the extra Alphabet Hunt page built and shown
for approval. Pages 2–5 NOT built yet.
Nothing is locked, approved, merged, or published. Branch: muse/halloween-fun-learning.

## Pack idea

A cheerful, friendly (never scary) Halloween pack. Bright original
watercolor picture-book illustrations, large activity areas, minimal text,
one clear learning objective per page.

## Pages

1. Halloween ABC (BUILT, awaiting approval; revised: instruction now reads
   "Draw a line from each picture to its beginning letter." and the
   illustrations are enlarged inside their existing frames, especially
   the bat and ghost): beginning-sound matching.
   Four friendly pictures (bat, pumpkin, ghost, cat) matched to their first
   letters (B, P, G, C, in mixed order P, C, G, B).
   Objective: hear the first sound in a Halloween word and match the
   picture to its beginning letter.
   Answers: bat to B, pumpkin to P, ghost to G, cat to C.
Extra page: Halloween Alphabet Hunt, A to Z (CORRECTED, awaiting approval):
   Letters-only hunt grid with all 26 uppercase letters exactly once in
   mixed-up order (rows of 7, 7, 6, 6); the 4 friendly Halloween pictures
   sit outside the grid as small decorations below it. Children find and
   circle each letter, then mark it on the enlarged A-Z checklist at the
   bottom (A-M, then N-Z, each box with a big letter and a big checkbox).
   Objective: recognize all 26 uppercase letters and track found letters
   on a checklist.
2. Count the Halloween Treats (1–10): count groups of Halloween treats and
   write the number in a big box.
   Objective: count groups of objects to 10 and record the number.
3. Complete the Halloween Patterns (AB, AAB, ABC): finish three Halloween
   pattern strips by drawing or choosing the next picture.
   Objective: recognize and extend AB, AAB, and ABC patterns.
4. Dress Up for Halloween (cut-and-paste): cut out chunky costume pieces
   and paste them onto a friendly character, with a separate WORK AREA and
   CUT-OUT AREA.
   Objective: follow a dressing task by cutting and pasting pieces in place.
5. Friendly Ghost Halloween Maze: help a friendly ghost find its way to a
   pumpkin through a simple maze with wide paths.
   Objective: follow a path through a simple maze from start to finish.

## Style rules for this pack

- Friendly faces only: smiling bat, pumpkin, ghost, and cat. Nothing scary.
- Original illustrations made for this pack; no copied artwork.
- Large pictures, large writing and drawing areas, short simple prompts.
- Brand header on every page (Learning Made Simple lockup).
- No em-dash anywhere.
