# Counting to 120 (Grade 1 Math, 1.NBT.A.1)

## Learning objective (parent-friendly)

Your child will count forward to 120, starting from any number, not just from 1.
They will fill in missing numbers in sequences up to 120, read and write the
numbers 101 to 120 as words and numerals, and finish by completing a 101 to 120
grid on their own.

## What is inside

- Page 1, Count On From Here: count on from 88, 97, 104, and 115.
- Page 2, What's Missing Past 100?: fill missing numbers in sequences that
  cross 100 and the decade boundaries.
- Page 3, Read It and Write It, 101 to 120: read number words and write the
  numeral, then write the number word for 101, 107, and 115 using the word bank.
- Page 4, Patterns Past 100: skip-counting patterns by 2s, 5s, and 10s that go
  past 100. This page is enrichment and extension practice; skip-counting
  beyond 100 is not required Grade 1 coverage, so treat it as a bonus challenge.
- Page 5, The 101 to 120 Challenge: fill the 101 to 120 grid, then try three
  mixed questions independently.

## Practical guidance

- Let your child say each number out loud as they write it. Hearing the
  sequence helps them catch a skipped number.
- The tricky spots are 99 to 101 and 109 to 111, where the hundreds digit
  changes. If your child stalls there, count that short stretch together, then
  let them continue alone.
- For Page 3, Part B, the word bank gives every word they need. The only new
  idea is "one hundred" plus the ones word.
- For Page 4, do not worry if the patterns feel hard. They are extra practice.
  Praise the attempt and move on if needed.
- The answer key is for grown-ups only. Please do not print it for children.
  Codex note: place the answer key as a parent-only asset, not a child-facing
  page.
- This pack builds on the Kindergarten counting-to-100 work and leads into the
  next Grade 1 pack, Tens and Ones to 100.
