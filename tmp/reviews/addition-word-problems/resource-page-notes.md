# Addition Word Problems Within 20

Grade 1 Math. Five printable pages plus a separate parent answer key.
Standards: 1.OA.A.1 (use addition within 20 to solve word problems) and
1.OA.A.2 (add three whole numbers whose sum is within 20).

## What this pack teaches

Addition word problems within 20, in a careful progression. Every problem
combines the same group of objects, so the total always has a clear unit.
The pack contains no subtraction and no two-step problems, by design.
Two-step problems belong in a separate activity.

## Page by page

Page 1, Add with Picture Groups. Result-unknown addition with picture
groups. Four problems: 7 red apples + 5 green apples = 12 apples;
6 blue balloons + 8 pink balloons = 14 balloons; 8 red cars + 3 blue
cars = 11 cars; 4 green frogs + 9 dark green frogs = 13 frogs.

Page 2, Add with Ten-Frames. Result-unknown addition with standard 2 by 5
ten-frames, dots filled left to right, top row first. Four problems:
9 + 4 = 13 marbles; 8 + 6 = 14 stickers; 7 + 5 = 12 apples;
6 + 9 = 15 pencils.

Page 3, Find the Missing Part. Change-unknown addition with labeled
part-part-whole models (Total, Have, More). Three problems: Maya has 6
crayons and ends with 14, so her friend gave her 8; Tom has 5 toy cars
and ends with 12, so his dad gave him 7; Noah has 3 robots and ends
with 16, so his brother gave him 13. The cars problem alternates the
unknown-part box position (More on the left, Have on the right) to show
that order does not change the total. A small worked example at the top
is tied to a short story: Sara has 4 pencils, then gets 6 more, and now
she has 10.

Page 4, Draw the Story. Start-unknown addition. Children draw the
starting group with dots or circles, not the final total, then write
how many there were at first. Three problems: birds, ? + 6 = 15, so 9
at first; blocks, ? + 7 = 18, so 11 at first; stickers, ? + 5 = 13,
so 8 at first. Each problem has an explicit At first ? + Added = Now
strip and a large drawing space.

Page 5, Add Three Numbers. Three-addend word problems within 20 with
scaffolding that fades across the page. Problem 1 (pencils, 4 + 5 + 6
= 15) gives pictures, a circle-the-make-10 prompt, and an equation.
Problem 2 (shells, 3 red + 7 blue + 8 white = 18) gives pictures and
an equation with no hints. Problem 3 (blocks, 8 + 2 + 9 = 19) gives the
story and an equation with no pictures.

## Answers

Page 1: 12 apples, 14 balloons, 11 cars, 13 frogs.
Page 2: 13 marbles, 14 stickers, 12 apples, 15 pencils.
Page 3: 8 crayons, 7 cars, 13 robots.
Page 4: 9 birds at first, 11 blocks at first, 8 stickers at first.
Page 5: 15 pencils (4 + 6 make 10), 18 shells, 19 blocks.
