# Learning Made Simple Curriculum Map

> Primary K–2 curriculum reference: Rochester Community Schools (RCS). Website navigation remains broad; this file tracks detailed curriculum coverage.

`[x]` means a matching resource pack currently exists in the repository. `[ ]` means planned or not yet clearly covered.

## Preschool

The Preschool curriculum plan below retains the current website subject and topic structure.

### Math

#### Numbers & Counting

- [x] Number Recognition 1–5 — recognize, find, count, and trace numbers 1–5
- [x] Number Recognition 6–10 — recognize, find, count, and trace numbers 6–10
- [x] Number Recognition 11–15 — recognize, find, count, and trace numbers 11–15
- [x] Number Recognition 16–20 — recognize, find, count, and trace numbers 16–20
- [x] Counting Objects 1–5 — count groups and connect quantities to numerals 1–5
- [x] Counting Objects 1–10 — count, match, write, and draw quantities 1–10
- [x] Counting Objects 11–20 — count, match, write, and draw quantities 11–20
- [x] Number Order 1–10 — before and after, missing numbers, and ordering 1–10
- [x] Number Order 11–20 — before and after, missing numbers, and ordering 11–20
- [x] More, Fewer & Same 1–10 — visually compare quantities as more, fewer, or the same

#### Early Addition & Subtraction

- [x] Addition to 5 — put together, count, complete, draw, and add with sums through 5
- [x] Subtracting Within 5 — take away, cross out, complete, draw, and subtract within 5
- [x] Addition Facts Within 5 — horizontal, vertical, fill-in, and mixed addition facts
- [x] Subtraction Facts Within 5 — horizontal, vertical, fill-in, and mixed subtraction facts

#### Shapes & Spatial Skills

- [ ] Preschool Shapes & Spatial Skills — identify, describe, build, and position simple shapes

#### Patterns

- [x] Preschool Patterns — copy, extend, and create simple repeating patterns

#### Measurement & Comparing

- [x] Preschool Measurement & Comparing — compare length, height, size, amounts, and relative size

#### Sorting & Data

- [x] Preschool Sorting & Data — classify objects and interpret simple picture data

### Reading & Language

- [ ] Alphabet — recognize, name, match, and form letters
- [ ] Print Awareness — book handling, print direction, words, and spaces
- [ ] Sound Awareness — rhyme, syllables, and beginning sounds
- [ ] Early Phonics — connect letters with common sounds
- [ ] Vocabulary — learn, sort, and use age-appropriate words
- [ ] Story & Comprehension — sequence, retell, and answer simple story questions
- [ ] Early Writing — pre-writing strokes, letter formation, labels, and simple messages

### Communication & Life Skills

- [ ] Understanding Language — follow directions and understand common concepts
- [ ] Expressing Needs & Ideas — request, choose, describe, and share ideas
- [ ] WH Questions — answer who, what, where, when, and why questions
- [ ] Conversation & Play — take turns, respond, and participate in play
- [ ] Feelings & Social Understanding — identify emotions and common social situations
- [ ] Visual Supports & Routines — follow visual schedules, routines, and transitions
- [ ] Independence & Safety — practice self-help, boundaries, and basic safety

### Science & Discovery

- [ ] Animals — identify animals, features, needs, and habitats
- [ ] Plants & Nature — identify plant parts, needs, growth, and natural objects
- [ ] Human Body & Five Senses — identify body parts and explore the senses
- [ ] Weather & Seasons — observe weather and seasonal changes
- [ ] Earth & Space — explore land, water, sky, sun, moon, and stars
- [ ] Living & Non-Living — sort and describe living and non-living things
- [ ] Exploring Materials — observe texture, flexibility, strength, and change
- [ ] Little Scientist — observe, predict, compare, and record simple findings

### Thinking & Our World

- [ ] Logic & Matching — match, classify, sequence, and solve simple visual problems
- [ ] Myself & Family — identity, family roles, similarities, and differences
- [ ] Community — community places, helpers, and shared rules
- [ ] Maps & Places — positional language, simple maps, and familiar places
- [ ] Time & Sequence — daily order, first/next/last, and past/present

## Kindergarten Math

### Numbers & Counting

- [ ] Counting to 100 — count by 1s and 10s to 100 and begin from different starting numbers
- [ ] Numeral Recognition 0–30 — recognize numerals in random order
- [ ] Counting Objects 1–20 — use one-to-one correspondence and answer “how many?”
- [ ] Compare Groups — identify greater, less, and equal groups
- [ ] Compare Numbers 1–10 — compare written numerals

### Addition & Subtraction

- [ ] Add & Subtract Within 10 — use objects, drawings, fingers, and equations
- [ ] Make and Break Numbers to 10 — compose and decompose quantities through 10
- [ ] Ways to Make 10 — find combinations with a total of 10
- [ ] Addition & Subtraction Stories — solve story problems and simple equations
- [ ] Fluency Within 5 — build quick recall of addition and subtraction facts within 5

### Place Value Foundations

- [ ] Teen Numbers: Ten and Ones — compose, decompose, represent, and write 11–19 as one ten plus ones

### Measurement & Data

- [ ] Compare Length, Height & Weight — visually and directly compare measurable attributes
- [ ] Sort, Classify & Count — classify objects and count objects within categories

### Geometry & Spatial

- [ ] Position Words — above, below, beside, in front, behind, and next to
- [ ] 2D Shape Names & Attributes — circle, square, rectangle, triangle, hexagon, and rhombus
- [ ] 3D Shape Names & Attributes — cube, cone, cylinder, and sphere
- [ ] 2D or 3D? — distinguish flat and solid shapes
- [ ] Compare, Build & Draw Shapes — compare attributes and create shapes
- [ ] Compose Shapes — combine simple shapes into larger shapes

## Grade 1 Math

### Numbers & Place Value

- [ ] Counting Patterns to 120 — count by 1s, 2s, 5s, and 10s from varied starting numbers
- [ ] Read, Write & Represent 0–120 — connect quantities, number names, and numerals
- [ ] Tens and Ones — understand 10 ones as 1 ten, teen numbers, and multiples of ten
- [ ] Compare & Order Numbers — use <, >, and =
- [ ] Ten More, Ten Less — mentally find 10 more or 10 less
- [ ] Subtract Tens — subtract multiples of 10

### Addition & Subtraction

- [ ] Word Problems Within 20 — solve addition and subtraction situations
- [ ] Add Three Numbers — add three whole numbers with totals through 20
- [ ] Related Addition & Subtraction — inverse operations, unknown-addend thinking, and fact families
- [ ] Addition Strategies — associative grouping and counting on
- [ ] Facts Through 10 + 10 — addition facts and related subtraction facts
- [ ] Equations & Equality — meaning of =, true/false equations, and missing numbers
- [ ] Add Within 100 — use models, drawings, and place-value strategies
- [ ] Add Tens and Ones — combine tens and ones and compose a new ten
- [ ] Explain Your Strategy — record and explain mathematical reasoning

### Measurement & Data

- [ ] Compare and Order Lengths — order three objects and compare lengths indirectly
- [ ] Measure with Non-Standard Units — repeat equal units without gaps or overlaps
- [ ] Time to Hour and Half-Hour — read and write analog and digital time
- [ ] Data with Three Categories — organize, represent, interpret, and compare category totals

### Geometry & Fractions

- [ ] Shape Attributes — distinguish defining and non-defining attributes
- [ ] Build, Draw & Compose Shapes — create 2D and 3D shapes from attributes
- [ ] Halves and Fourths — partition into equal shares and name halves, fourths, and quarters
- [ ] Equal Shares and Size — understand that more equal shares make smaller shares

## Grade 2 Math

### Operations & Algebraic Thinking

- [ ] Word Problems Within 100 — solve one-step and two-step addition and subtraction problems
- [ ] Open & Missing-Value Equations — find unknown values in equations
- [ ] Fluency Within 20 — add and subtract accurately and mentally within 20
- [ ] Equal Groups and Odd or Even — form equal groups and identify odd/even numbers through 20
- [ ] Arrays & Repeated Addition — build arrays and write matching addition equations

### Numbers & Place Value

- [ ] Hundreds, Tens & Ones — understand place value and 10 tens as 1 hundred
- [ ] Count and Skip Count to 1,000 — count and skip count by 5s, 10s, and 100s
- [ ] Read & Write to 1,000 — use numerals, number names, and expanded form
- [ ] Compare Three-Digit Numbers — use <, >, and =
- [ ] Add & Subtract Within 100 — use place-value strategies with and without regrouping
- [ ] Add Four Two-Digit Numbers — add up to four two-digit addends
- [ ] Add & Subtract Within 1,000 — use models, drawings, and strategies
- [ ] Mental 10 and 100 — mentally add or subtract 10 or 100

### Measurement

- [ ] Measure & Estimate Length — use rulers, yardsticks, meter sticks, and measuring tapes
- [ ] Standard Length Units — work with inches, feet, centimeters, and meters
- [ ] Compare Measurements — compare results from different units and find length differences
- [ ] Measurement Word Problems — solve addition and subtraction situations involving length
- [ ] Number-Line Sums & Differences — solve within 100 on a number line

### Time & Money

- [ ] Time to Five Minutes — read analog and digital time and use a.m./p.m.
- [ ] Coins and Dollar Bills — identify and count dollars, quarters, dimes, nickels, and pennies
- [ ] Money Problems & Symbols — solve money problems using $ and ¢

### Data

- [ ] Measurement Data & Line Plots — generate measurements and represent them on line plots
- [ ] Picture and Bar Graphs — represent up to four categories
- [ ] Graph Problems — solve put-together, take-apart, and comparison questions

### Geometry & Equal Shares

- [ ] Shape Names & Attributes — triangles, quadrilaterals, pentagons, hexagons, and cubes
- [ ] Draw Shapes from Attributes — identify and create shapes from defining features
- [ ] Rows, Columns & Equal Squares — partition rectangles into equal square arrays
- [ ] Halves, Thirds & Fourths — partition shapes into named equal shares
- [ ] Equal Shares in Different Shapes — recognize equal shares even when their shapes differ
