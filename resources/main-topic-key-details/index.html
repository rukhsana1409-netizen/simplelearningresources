For adults

These are nonfiction texts: they tell true facts, not stories. If your child is stuck, point back to the passage: every answer is in the text. The main topic is what the whole text is about, not just one fact. Page 1 is bats (they fly at night, eat bugs, sleep in the day); Page 2 is how a butterfly grows (egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, butterfly); Page 3 is your heart (in your chest, pumps blood, beats fast when you run and play); Page 4 is the Moon (the Sun lights it up, and it looks different each night as it moves around the Earth); Page 5 is trains (tracks, engine and cars, stations). Use the separate parent answer key to check the fixed answers.
