For adults: use a first name or nickname only, and do not include personal information about a child.

Learning focus

  • Describe a thing you love out loud, then write two complete describing sentences about it.
  • Draw a place you love, then write what you can see and hear there.
  • Describe a special person, how they look and how they make you feel, then draw them.
  • Choose your own person, place, thing, or event, draw it, and write about it.
  • Use your drawing to help you tell someone about it, sharing three things.

For adults

Let your child pick a familiar person, place, thing, or event. On each page, ask them to describe it out loud first, then write and draw. The sentence frames fade across the pack: full frames on page 1, shorter frames on page 2, complete starters on page 3, and open lines on page 4, so your child builds toward writing their own describing sentences. This pack builds two Grade 1 speaking and listening goals: describing a person, place, thing, or event with relevant details in complete sentences (SL.1.4), and adding drawings to clarify ideas (SL.1.5). On page 4, encourage your child to show their drawing to someone and tell them three things about it.
