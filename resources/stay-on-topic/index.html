Learning focus

  • Identify what a conversation is about and distinguish responses that connect to the shared topic.
  • Keep a conversation going by adding a relevant statement or asking a related question.

For adults

Read each conversation aloud and invite your child to explain how each response connects. Page 1 answers are dog, bicycle, and soccer ball. On page 2, circle the shells, falling sand, red bucket, and river comments; mark the dog and pancakes comments with an X. Pages 3 and 4 are open-ended: accept the child's own relevant statement or question, and on page 4 either following the new ice-cream topic or adding one final kite comment is appropriate.
