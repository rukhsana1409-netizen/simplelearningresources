For adults

Keep this pack oral-first: say each prompt clearly and let your child answer by speaking, pointing, circling, or drawing dots. Page 1 alternates long/short for cake, cat, kite, kit, rope, rock, tube, tub; Page 2 answers are stop, flag, snip, bridge, clap, stand; Page 3 beginning/middle/end sounds are flag /f/ /a/ /g/, stop /s/ /o/ /p/, milk /m/ /i/ /k/, nest /n/ /e/ /t/, clip /k/ /i/ /p/, clap /k/ /a/ /p/; Page 4 has 4 sounds in clap, tent, and milk and 5 in stamp, stand, and blimp.
