Learning focus

  • Identify everyday actions that can help protect land, water, air, and living things.
  • Communicate a simple solution to an environmental problem observed at home, school, or outdoors.

For adults

Name what is happening in each picture and ask who or what the action helps or harms before sorting. For the final page, help your child choose a real, familiar problem and explain how their proposed action could help, without implying that one small action solves the whole environmental issue.
