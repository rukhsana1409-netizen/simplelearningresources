Learning focus

  • Listen or look for who a message is directed to, then respond in a way that connects to what the other person communicated.
  • Practice relevant replies to questions and comments using spoken words, pointing, gestures, or another reliable communication method.

For adults

Read each message aloud and give your child time to answer by speaking, pointing, or showing. Page 1 answers are Alex, the class, Mia, and the drawing; on page 2, match “My rock collection!” to what was brought, the pointing picture to where the item goes, and either polite grape response to the offer. On page 3, circle responses that stay connected to the tower or seedlings; all three tooth responses are appropriate. Page 4 is open-ended, so accept any relevant response in the child's own communication style.
