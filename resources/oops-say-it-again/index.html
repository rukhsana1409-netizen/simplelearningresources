For adults

Read each situation aloud and accept more than one helpful response, including speaking, showing, pointing, or drawing. On Page 2 the helpful questions are “Which one?”, “Where?”, and “What do you mean?”; on Pages 3–4, invite your child to explain or demonstrate a respectful way to correct the mix-up in their own words.
