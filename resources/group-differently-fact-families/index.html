For adults

Use this supplement after Addition Fact Strategies Within 20, Subtraction Fact Strategies Within 20, and Unknown Numbers & True or False Equations. Ask your child to compare both groupings on Page 1 and explain why the total stays the same; on Page 2, have them say the related addition facts aloud before completing each subtraction fact.
