Learning focus

  • Use picture clues and everyday knowledge to answer who, what, where, when, why, and how questions.
  • Give relevant answers through speaking, pointing, drawing, or writing.

For adults

Read each question aloud and accept any clear answer supported by the picture or the child's experience; speaking, pointing, drawing, and writing are all appropriate. Suggested picture answers: page 1—the woman is reading, the girl is flying a red kite, the dog is resting under the tree/on the grass, and the boy is watering flowers to help them grow; page 2—the older woman gives the child a book, the man washes carrots at the kitchen sink, and the children plant seeds in a garden bed. Pages 3 and 4 allow reasonable personal answers, such as using an umbrella when it rains, wearing a coat to stay warm, watering a plant to help it grow, and asking for help when something is difficult or unsafe.
