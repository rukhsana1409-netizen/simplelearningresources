Learning focus

For adults

The feelings are never named in the stories, so ask how a character's face and actions show how they feel. Page 1: Maya is kind because she shares her crayons with Ana. Page 2: Theo is frustrated when the puzzle piece will not fit, then happy when it fits. Page 3: Theo is responsible because he puts the red mitten in the lost and found. Page 4: Maya is scared at the start and proud at the end, because she reads her story and the class claps. Page 5: Theo is flexible because he moves the rainy picnic inside. Use the separate parent answer key to check the fixed answers.
