Learning focus

  • Plan and carry out simple investigations to compare how different strengths or directions of pushes and pulls change an object's motion.
  • Record observations and measurements from simple motion tests, building evidence for later design work about changing an object's speed or direction.

For adults

Use the same object, surface, ramp, and starting point when the page asks for a fair comparison, changing only the strength or direction of the push. Invite your child to predict first, measure or draw what happened, and describe the evidence in their own words; supervise each hands-on test and choose safe objects and spaces.
