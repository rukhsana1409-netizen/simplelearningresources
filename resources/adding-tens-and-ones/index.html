For adults

Ask your child to name the tens and ones before adding and explain which place changes. When 10 or more ones are combined, use the worksheet language: circle 10 ones, exchange them for 1 new ten, then count the remaining tens and ones; avoid introducing a carried-digit shortcut before the model makes sense.
