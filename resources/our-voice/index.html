Learning focus

  • Express an opinion about a shared classroom concern and give a simple reason for that choice.
  • Participate in planning and reflecting on an age-appropriate project that helps the classroom or community.

For adults

Read the choices aloud and invite your child to explain an idea in their own words. These activities are open-ended: on page 1, either choice is acceptable when supported by a reason; page 2 records the child's selected project and plan; page 3 should be completed after the real project and may be written by an adult using the child's exact words.
