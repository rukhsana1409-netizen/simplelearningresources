Pushes & Pulls — Stop or Turn!

Learning focus

  • Design and test a simple structure intended to change a rolling ball's motion by stopping it or making it turn.
  • Use repeated observations to decide whether a design worked and identify when it should be changed and tested again.

For adults

Help your child keep the ball, ramp or gentle push, and starting point the same for all three tests so the comparison is fair. Ask what the ball actually did after each trial, record that evidence, and encourage one design change and another test when the first attempt does not work.
