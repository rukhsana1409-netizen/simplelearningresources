Learning focus

  • Recognize that children can join conversations through speech, pointing, waving, AAC, or drawing.
  • Practice listening, taking turns, inviting others respectfully, accepting yes or no, and adding a connected response.

For adults

Read each scene aloud and accept speech, pointing, AAC, gestures, or drawing as valid participation. Page 1 answers are Alex—say, Mia—point, and Friend—talker. On page 2, star the child whose attention is directed toward the speaker. On page 3, choose “Want to build with us?”, either “Yes, please!” or “No, thank you!”, and “Okay, have fun!” Page 4 is open-ended; accept any response that connects to the robin or dinosaur conversation.
