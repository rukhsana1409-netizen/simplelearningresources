Learning focus

  • Use frequently occurring conjunctions, including and, but, or, so, and because (L.1.1(g)).
  • Use determiners such as a, the, this, and that (L.1.1(h)).
  • Produce and expand complete simple and compound declarative, interrogative, imperative, and exclamatory sentences (L.1.1(j)).
  • Use words and phrases learned through conversations, reading, and responding to texts (L.1.6).

For adults

Joining words help your child say more in one sentence. Help your child hear what and, but, or, so, and because mean. Talk about a, the, this, and that in books you read together. Ask what a sentence does: does it tell, ask, or tell someone to do something? Then let your child build a sentence of their own.
