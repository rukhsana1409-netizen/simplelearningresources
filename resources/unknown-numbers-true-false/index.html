For adults

Ask your child to work out both sides before deciding whether an equation is true, and invite them to explain what the equal sign means. For missing numbers, read the entire equation aloud and let your child use drawings, counters, or mental reasoning; on the final page, accept any one-number change that makes the false equation true.
