Learning focus

  • Pick one familiar how-to and name it, then write the book title.
  • Draw the four steps in order using First, Next, Then, and Last.
  • Write one sentence per step using the time words, with a starter for the first two steps.
  • Close the book with a closing line and add one true fact or helpful tip.
  • Check the book with a simple self-check.

For adults

Let your child pick one how-to they know well, washing hands, making a sandwich, planting a seed, or brushing teeth. Help them name the steps in order, write each step with a time word, and add one true fact or helpful tip. This pack builds two Grade 1 writing goals: writing an informative piece that names a topic, supplies facts, and closes the piece (W.1.2), and recalling information from experience to answer a question (W.1.8). If your child cannot think of a how-to, offer two familiar ones and let them choose one.
