Learning focus

  • Organize data into up to three categories, represent it with tally tables and picture graphs, and answer questions about totals, category counts, and how many more or fewer.

For adults

On the graphing pages, reinforce that one picture or colored box represents one vote. For the final survey, your child may ask 1 to 10 people; help them total the three counts, and accept both fruit names if two categories tie for the most votes.
