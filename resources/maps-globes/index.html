Learning focus

  • Recognize a globe as a model of Earth and a map as a drawing that can show places from above.
  • Use simple map features and connect familiar community places and natural environments with their purposes and resources.

For adults

Read the short explanations aloud, then connect each activity to places your child knows. On the school map, trace a route with a finger before talking through a similar route at your child's school; accept observations in the child's own words and help with drawing or matching as needed.
