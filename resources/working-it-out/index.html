Learning focus

  • Identify how people in a shared problem may feel and explain that more than one interpretation can be reasonable.
  • Use words, taking turns, sharing, or adult help to work through simple conflicts in kind and fair ways.

For adults

Read each situation aloud and accept different feeling words when your child can explain the visual clue or story detail behind the choice. Encourage your child to suggest kind, fair solutions in their own words; on the final page, write their exact spoken ending rather than requiring a perfect sentence.
