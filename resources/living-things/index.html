Learning focus

  • Use pictures and observations to describe what plants and animals need to live.
  • Identify evidence that living things can change their surroundings and record plant growth over time.

For adults

Ask your child to point to picture evidence before explaining each answer, and record their exact words when the page invites an oral response. During the seed observation, keep the growing conditions consistent and record what is actually seen and measured without promising a particular result.
