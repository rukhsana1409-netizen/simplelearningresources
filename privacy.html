Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Learning Made Simple provides free educational worksheets and printable resources. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit simplelearningresources.com, download a resource, or contact us.

Who This Website Is For

Learning Made Simple is intended primarily for parents, teachers, caregivers, and other adults seeking educational resources for children. Children may use the worksheets with appropriate adult guidance, but the website does not offer child accounts, comments, messaging, contests, or other features that ask children to submit personal information.

Children should not send names, email addresses, photographs, school information, or other personal information through the website or by email. A parent or responsible adult should contact us on a child's behalf.

Current Website Practices

You do not need an account or need to provide personal information to browse or download resources. The website does not currently use Google AdSense or Google Analytics and does not currently set advertising cookies through those services. Website search runs in your browser and does not require an account.

When you visit, the companies that host, deliver, and protect the website may process technical information needed to provide those services. This can include your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages or files, request date and time, referring page, approximate location derived from an IP address, and security or diagnostic information. Learning Made Simple does not use this information to create visitor profiles.

Information You Choose to Provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information included in your message, such as your email address, name, message, and any attachment you choose to send. Please do not send sensitive information or personal information about a child. We use correspondence to respond, maintain necessary records, address security or legal matters, and protect the website. We retain it only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or as required by law.

Current Service Providers

GitHub Pages hosts the website's static files. GitHub may process request and technical information needed to provide and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cloudflare provides storage or delivery services for worksheet PDFs and preview images served from assets.simplelearningresources.com. Cloudflare may process request details, IP addresses, and security information needed to deliver and protect those files. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

If you email us, your email provider and our email provider process the message under their own privacy policies.

Planned Advertising

Google AdSense is not currently active on this website. If advertising is activated, Learning Made Simple plans to apply child age treatment across the site and request contextual or otherwise restricted, non-personalized ads. We do not plan to permit personalized advertising or remarketing.

Under this planned configuration, Google may process information such as IP addresses, browser or device information, page context, cookies, web beacons, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, prevent fraud and abuse, and produce aggregated reports. Non-personalized ads are based on context rather than a visitor's past browsing behavior, but they may still use cookies or similar identifiers for frequency control, aggregated reporting, security, and fraud prevention.

Child age treatment is intended to disable personalized ads and remarketing and apply Google's child-related advertising protections. It does not remove all technical processing involved in serving an ad. Before advertising is activated, this policy will be reviewed against the final configuration and updated if necessary. See Google's advertising information and Privacy Policy.

Cookies, Consent, and Your Choices

Learning Made Simple does not currently use advertising cookies. If advertising is activated, Google may use cookies, local storage, pixels, IP addresses, and browser or device information for the restricted purposes described above.

Where applicable law requires consent before cookies, local storage, or advertising identifiers are used, we plan to present an appropriate consent or privacy message before making the relevant advertising request. No consent-management system is currently active.

You can use your browser settings to block or delete cookies and use any privacy controls that may later appear on the website. You may also review Google Ads Settings. Blocking cookies should not prevent access to the website's core worksheets and educational content.

Children's Privacy

Learning Made Simple does not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information. Because the educational content is intended for young children, we use a conservative privacy approach. If advertising is activated, we plan to apply child age treatment site-wide and use contextual or restricted, non-personalized advertising rather than behavioral advertising or remarketing.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to Learning Made Simple, contact us. We will review the request and delete the information when appropriate. Parents and caregivers should supervise children's use of the website and contact us on a child's behalf.

How Information Is Shared

Learning Made Simple does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by providers that host, deliver, secure, or maintain the website; by email providers when you contact us; when reasonably necessary to address fraud or security incidents; or when required by law. If Google AdSense is activated, Google may process information for the restricted advertising purposes described in this policy.

Retention and Security

We retain information under our direct control only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was received, for security and recordkeeping, or as required by law. Service providers maintain their own retention and security practices. We use reasonable safeguards, but no Internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

External Links

The website may link to third-party websites that have their own privacy practices. Learning Made Simple does not control those websites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers, advertising plans, or legal obligations change. The date at the top will show the latest revision. If advertising is activated, we will update this policy to reflect the active configuration.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Learning Made Simple at hello@simplelearningresources.com.
